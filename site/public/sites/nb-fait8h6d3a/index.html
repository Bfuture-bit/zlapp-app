Why trust it?

This prototype separates a real funded job from a financeable receivable and tells you exactly where the evidence stops.

PHMSA SF-271 bridge signal

PHMSA construction reimbursement happens after eligible cost is posted; recipients use SF-271. This is a reason to investigate timing—not proof that a contractor is unpaid.

PHMSA rule ↗

DOE reimbursement signal

GRIP reimbursement requests generally track incurred/disbursed costs and cannot be submitted more often than every two weeks. Award-specific payment terms control.

DOE rule ↗

No bridge without legal collateral proof

EcoSmart should require an exact earned amount, acceptance evidence, actual obligor/payment source, agreement-specific assignability, bond/surety review, UCC/collateral priority, expected payment and borrower request before sizing capital.
